---

I build native apps for Apple platforms in Swift and SwiftUI, ship cross-platform in Flutter, and own the backend and infrastructure those apps depend on.

My career started one layer below — firmware, PCBs, machined parts, a soil probe buried in the field. The Apple Developer Academy was the hinge into app engineering, and that background is why I stay comfortable all the way down the stack.

### What I own today

- **3D & spatial pipeline (macOS · visionOS)** — a STEP/STP · FBX · OBJ · JT · GLB → USDZ converter, with the OpenCascade tessellation pipeline written by me, and Gaussian Splatting that turns photos of a space into a 3D scene.
- **Infrastructure & release** — test, UAT and production environments, plus the deploy and release pipelines. Architecture and infra decisions go through me, and I run the backend team's board.
- **Flutter at enterprise scale** — through my own consultancy, frontend engineer on a connected-vehicle product used worldwide, owning the Plug &amp; Charge (ISO 15118) feature.
- **Tradx** — co-founder &amp; COO of a Flutter trading platform on the Mac App Store and Microsoft Store, ~15k downloads across both.

### Along the way

- Part-time **tech lead** for a 12-person residency cohort, four months after becoming a junior — then chose the technical track over management.
- During a **UIKit → SwiftUI** migration, designed the team's rotating code-review queue and automated it in CI/CD.
- **30+ paying freelance clients** in agriculture and automotive parts: PCBs with sensor and power redundancy, a 5-node LoRa mesh reaching ~2 km in open field.
